About

The Computational Mechanics of Soft Materials Group develops mathematical models, numerical methods, and scientific software for soft solids, surface mechanics, instabilities, contact, and multiphysics problems.

The group combines nonlinear continuum mechanics, variational modeling, finite element methods, and open-source implementation, with applications in advanced soft materials and structures.
